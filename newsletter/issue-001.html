Tally, for every form you'll need this year

Waitlists, onboarding questions, feedback surveys, client briefs. Tally builds them like a doc: type a question, hit enter, done. The free plan is generous enough that most early companies never upgrade.

The job it does: put a 3-question form behind every "contact us" and "join the waitlist" button. You'll learn who your visitors are before you ever talk to them.

The paid analytics plan you don't have traffic for

Premium analytics suite at a few hundred visitors a month

At that traffic, a premium suite tells you little a free plan can't. Downgrade to the free tier of PostHog or Google Analytics and watch one number each week: how many visitors became signups or leads.

Upgrade again when: you're running experiments and need more data than the free plan keeps.

✦ AI WORKFLOW

Answer templates for your inbox

setup: 20 min · tools: Claude + Notion · level: chat

You answer the same ten questions every week: pricing, timelines, "does it work with…". Write the answers once, in your own voice.

01Search your sent mail and list the 10 questions you answer most.
02Copy two emails you've written that sound like you.
03Paste both into Claude with this prompt:
Here are 10 questions my customers often ask, and two emails I wrote so you can learn my tone.

For each question, write a reply template in my voice: under 120 words, friendly and direct, with [brackets] where I need to add details like a name or date. Then list any question where you need more information from me to answer well.

QUESTIONS: [paste]
MY EMAILS: [paste]
04Fix anything that doesn't sound like you, then save the templates in a Notion page called "Replies".
05Next time one of those questions lands: copy, fill the brackets, send.
